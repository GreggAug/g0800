To Generate Simulation:

iverilog -o [OUTPUT_NAME].vvp [input1].vvp [input2].vvp

Simulation execution:
vvp [SIM_NAME].vvp



To show circuit:
# Gate level:
yosys -p "read_verilog file.v; hierarchy -check; synth; show module_name"

# Abstracted level
yosys -p "read_verilog [file.v]; hierarchy -check; proc; opt; show"
